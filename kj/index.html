Four letters and you're racing

Make a room and send its code, a link or a QR code. Up to four kangaroos hop to the flag throwing boomerangs, rocks and Boss Maggie at the leader. Roobots take any empty seat, so a race is always ready, and Android and iPhone players race in the same rooms.

A hit costs time, never a life. Rooms are private and there is no chat.

Or jump from your wrist

On Wear OS watches every level is free, with no ads. Tap to jump, or switch on arm-motion controls and lift your arm instead.

Free to play. The phone game shows ads, and a single Remove ads purchase turns them off and changes nothing else. No account needed.
